I am submitting a follow-up complaint regarding Central Research , Inc. ( CRI ) and the servicing of my federal student loan. This complaint is in response to CRIs recent reply to my prior CFPB complaint, which I believe is incomplete, unsupported, and non-compliant with servicing obligations. Summary of the Situation : CRI previously acknowledged that my repayment plan was incorrectly configured during the transfer of my loans from XXXX, resulting in a miscalculated monthly payment. They unilaterally corrected this error and increased my monthly payment from {$140.00} to {$380.00}. In response to my initial CFPB complaint, CRI stated that : No additional interest or increase was assessed as a result of this correction. However, they did not provide any documentation or analysis to support this claim. ________________________________________ Key Issues with CRIs Response 1. Unsupported Assertion Regarding Interest CRI claims that no additional interest accrued due to their servicing error, yet : They did not provide pre- and post-correction amortization schedules They did not provide a calculation demonstrating how this conclusion was reached The table included in their response ( listing balances and payment amounts ) does not substantiate their claim Without supporting documentation, this statement is unverifiable and insufficient. ________________________________________ 2. Failure to Provide Requested Documentation I explicitly requested : A detailed breakdown of the recalculation of my payment Documentation supporting the correction A full accounting of any financial impact ( including interest ) CRI has not provided this information. ________________________________________ 3. Refusal to Communicate in Writing When I followed up to request clarification and documentation, CRI : Refused to provide further details in writing Directed me to call I have clearly requested written communication to maintain accurate records. CRIs refusal to provide written responses prevents transparency and undermines my ability to verify the accuracy of their claims. ________________________________________ Why This Is a Problem CRI has acknowledged a servicing error, yet has not demonstrated that my account was properly corrected They have made unsupported claims regarding the absence of financial harm They are refusing to provide written documentation or explanation, despite repeated requests This leaves me in a position where : My monthly payment has significantly increased I have no verifiable evidence that I was not financially harmed I am unable to obtain proper documentation from the servicer
