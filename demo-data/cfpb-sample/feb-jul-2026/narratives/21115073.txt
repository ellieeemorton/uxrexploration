This complaint concerns Navient Student Loan Servicing engaging in unauthorized thirdparty contact in connection with my student loan account. Navient contacted my neighbor by telephone in an attempt to locate or discuss my student loan. My neighbor has no affiliation with my loan and has never been listed as a reference, cosigner, or authorized contact. As a result of this contact, my neighbor came to my residence on two separate occasions attempting to reach me. He provided me with a piece of paper that he stated was what wah told to him by the phone call. The paper contained a callback telephone number, the name of a Navient representative, and an account number, and included the statement tell her it is good news. Navient spoke directly with my neighbor and represented the call as relating to good news. My neighbor wrote down the information during the call. When he questioned the Navient representative about why he was being contacted, the representative stated, We called all the houses around her house until someone answered. At no time did I authorize Navient to contact third parties regarding my account. My neighbor was never authorized to receive communications, messages, or information about my student loan. After learning of this incident, I contacted Navient to report the unauthorized thirdparty contact and advised them that their actions were inappropriate and unauthorized. Navient denied any wrongdoing and asserted that contacting my neighbor in this manner did not constitute a violation. This conduct caused significant embarrassment and distress and, in my understanding, constitutes a violation of applicable consumer protection, privacy, and fair debt collection requirements. As of XX/XX/XXXX, I remain concerned that unauthorized thirdparty contacts may continue. I am requesting that Navient immediately cease all thirdparty contact, confirm that my privacy will be respected going forward, and ensure full compliance with all applicable consumer protection and debt collection laws.
