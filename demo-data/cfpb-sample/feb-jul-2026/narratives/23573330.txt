I am submitting this complaint regarding my federal student loan servicer, CRI ( Central Research Inc. ), in connection with a goodwill deletion request that was denied. I missed payments on my federal student loans due to an unintentional oversight I did not have autopay set up. This was an isolated incident and not a reflection of my payment history or financial responsibility. Upon discovering the multiple missed payments, I immediately brought all accounts current and enrolled in autopay to prevent recurrence. I then contacted CRI to request a goodwill adjustment, and was told via email that they are unable to remove delinquent marks or complete goodwill adjustments under any circumstances. My concern is twofold : 1. Because federal student loans are reported as multiple separate tradelines, this single missed payment resulted in multiple delinquency marks on my credit report causing a disproportionate drop in my credit score. In my case, this singular student loan payment displays to the credit bureau as TWELVE loans. 2. CRIs blanket refusal to consider goodwill adjustments does not appear to align with standard industry practice, where servicers routinely exercise discretion in isolated, unintentional cases for borrowers who are otherwise in good standing. I am requesting that the CFPB review this matter and encourage CRI to reconsider a goodwill deletion across all affected loan tradelines. I have documentation of my correspondence with CRI included in this complaint. CRI responded with a copy/paste auto reply as can be noted by receiving the exact same response to my emails.
