I am submitting this complaint regarding a private student loan currently held by Navy Federal Credit Union and serviced by XXXX. Both the borrower and the cosigner on this loan are United States military veterans who have been formally determined by the Department of Veterans Affairs to be 100 % Permanently and Totally ( P & T ) disabled. These determinations reflect permanent, service-connected conditions that preclude the ability to maintain substantially gainful employment. Despite providing official VA disability documentation for both parties, the lender and servicer have failed to offer any meaningful, long-term resolution. The only options presented have been repeated short-term forbearance or payment deferrals, which continue to accrue interest and significantly increase the total loan balance. This approach does not take into account the permanent nature of the borrowers disabilities and instead creates a cycle of compounding debt with no realistic path toward repayment. Both borrower and cosigner are living on fixed disability income, and the expectation of eventual repayment under these conditions is not reasonable. Importantly, approximately {$20000.00} has already been paid toward this loan in good faith, including during periods of active service. The current circumstances are the result of permanent service-connected disabilities, not financial mismanagement. The continued refusal to evaluate or offer a permanent resolution raises serious concerns regarding : Good faith servicing practices Failure to reasonably consider documented permanent hardship The fairness of continued debt enforcement under conditions of total disability The absence of any disability-based relief or accommodation pathways
