On or about XX/XX/XXXX, my federal student loan servicer, Aidvantage, sent me a letter informing me that my loans had been placed into an administrative forbearance due to the federal court injunction affecting the SAVE repayment plan. That letter contained two specific representations : First, it stated : " While you are in this forbearance no payment is required on your account and your interest rate will be set to 0 %. This means no interest will accrue while you are in the forbearance. '' Second, it committed to direct notification : " We will notify you before the forbearance comes to an end. We do not currently have a time estimate. We will then send you your next monthly billing statement at least 21 days before your next monthly payment is due. '' I relied on both of these written representations. According to publicly available information, the Department of Education directed that interest began accruing on SAVE administrative forbearance accounts effective XX/XX/XXXX. My account carries a total balance of {$430000.00} across 20 active loans with interest rates ranging from 3.40 % to 8.05 %. At full accrual rates, my account generates approximately {$65.00} to {$75.00} per day in interest charges. Despite this material change to my account terms, Aidvantage did not notify me. I first learned that interest had resumed accruing through public news reporting, not through any communication from my servicer.
