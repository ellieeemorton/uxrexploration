I have several federal student loans that are currently in default. I contacted my loan servicer, Nelnet, to request a complete payment history for these loans. Specifically, I asked for the following information : The dates and amounts of all payments I have made. How those payments were applied ( e.g., to principal, interest, or fees ). An explanation of how the current outstanding balance was calculated. The Nelnet representative stated that they do not have any payment history for my loans and could not provide the requested information. However, my data from the National Student Loan Data System ( XXXX ) shows a different total outstanding balance than what Nelnet is reporting. The XXXX lists specific outstanding principal and interest balances for each loan, but Nelnet has been unable to explain how their balance was calculated or how it differs from the federal government 's official records. This inability to provide a payment history or a clear calculation of my balance has made it impossible for me to verify the amount owed, resolve the default status, or enter into a repayment agreement. I believe this is a violation of federal servicing requirements that mandate the maintenance of a borrower 's payment history and transparent accounting of loan balances.
