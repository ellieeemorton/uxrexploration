I recently discovered a defaulted federal student loan when it appeared on my credit report while I was in the process of trying to buy a home. I was previously unaware that this loan had gone into default and immediately attempted to resolve the issue so I could qualify for a mortgage. When I contacted the Department of Educations Default Resolution Group, I was told that paying the loan in full would remove the default from my credit report. Based on this information, I agreed to make a payment. After learning that this information was incorrect, I contacted them again before the payment processed and requested that it be canceled. The representative confirmed that the payment had been canceled. Despite this confirmation, the payment was still processed and withdrawn. I am now being told it will take approximately 45 days for review and additional time to receive a refund. This situation involves misinformation, failure to honor a confirmed cancellation, and the withdrawal of funds after cancellation was confirmed. I am requesting an expedited refund and a formal review of this error. This delay is directly impacting my ability to move forward with a home purchase.
