Throughout this process, I have acted in good faith and have fully cooperated with every request for information and documentation. I have submitted all documents requested of me and have repeatedly sought copies of the records being used to support the alleged debt. Despite my efforts, I have been denied access to the underlying documentation and have been unable to obtain meaningful review of my dispute. Federal Student Aid informed me that it had received documentation from an educational institution allegedly demonstrating that I attended the institution and received federal financial aid. I was never provided with the original records, source documents, attachments, enrollment records, financial aid applications, promissory notes, disbursement records, attendance records, or any other documentation necessary to verify the legitimacy of the alleged debt. The most troubling aspect of this matter is that I independently contacted the institution identified as the source of the alleged records.
