I am filing this complaint regarding inaccurate reporting and improper servicing of my federal student loan. My loan was placed back into repayment after a period of deferment, however I was never properly notified that my deferment had ended or that payments had resumed. The servicer relied on obsolete contact information including phone numbers and email addresses that have not been valid for over ten years. Because proper notification was never provided, I was unaware that payments had resumed and the account was reported as late and eventually defaulted. Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act and the Higher Education Act servicing requirements, furnishers must report accurate information and provide appropriate notice regarding repayment status changes. Because proper notice was not provided, the late payment reporting is inaccurate and resulted from a servicing failure. I am requesting a full review of the servicing history and notification records. I also request retroactive forbearance be applied for the entire period of missed payments and that all associated late payment reporting be corrected to reflect current or on-time status. Additionally, I request that the servicer provide documented proof of all notification attempts regarding the end of my deferment and the resumption of repayment. This should include dated records of mailed notices, electronic communications, or any other documented borrower contact attempts. If the servicer can not produce verifiable records demonstrating that proper notice was provided to a valid address or contact method, then the reported delinquency and default status can not be considered accurate. In that case, I request that retroactive forbearance be applied and that all related late payment reporting be corrected or removed from my credit file.
