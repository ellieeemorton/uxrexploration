I am a federal student loan borrower whose loans are serviced by Aidvantage. On XX/XX/year>, I became aware of circumstances suggesting that my nonpublic personal information may have been subject to unauthorized disclosure. I contacted Aidvantage to request confirmation of whether any unauthorized access to or transmission of my borrower data had occurred, identification of which corporate entity within the Maximus corporate family maintains custody and control of my nonpublic personal information, and a description of the administrative, technical, and physical safeguards in place to protect my data as required under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act and Regulation P ( 12 C.F.R. Part 1016 ). As of the date of this complaint more than XXXX weeks after my initial inquiry Aidvantage has not responded. Under GLBA and Regulation P, financial institutions are required to provide clear and conspicuous privacy notices describing their information-sharing practices and to maintain reasonable safeguards for consumer financial information. A servicer 's refusal or failure to respond to a borrower 's direct inquiry about the security of their personal information raises concerns about whether the servicer is meeting its obligations under federal privacy and data security requirements. My concern is heightened by the fact that Aidvantage operates through multiple affiliated entities including Maximus Federal Services , Inc. and Maximus XXXX, LLC under a single trade name. It is unclear to me as a consumer which entity is the data controller responsible for safeguarding my information, which entity would bear notification obligations in the event of a breach, and whether adequate data segregation controls exist across these affiliates. I have been unable to obtain this information through direct inquiry. I am not filing this complaint about any pending legal dispute. I am filing this complaint because I have a right as a consumer to understand how my nonpublic personal information is being safeguarded and to receive a response when I raise data security concerns with my loan servicer. Aidvantage 's silence on these questions is itself a regulatory concern.
