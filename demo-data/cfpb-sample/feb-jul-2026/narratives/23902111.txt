I did not sign up for any student loans at the beginning of my college career back in 2011. I was very adamant on not attending schools if I personally had to take out any loans and stressed that time and time again to my family and everyone in financial aid offices and to guidance counselors at the time. The financial advisors spoke with my mom and informed her she could take out a loan on my behalf to pay for my school and board at XXXX XXXX XXXX. Upon me leaving XXXX about a year and a half later I find out that the loan was actually taken out in my name as well as my mothers, had I been aware of this I would have never attended the school because I did not want to be in debt before even graduating. My mom who was also knew to this process, apparently just took the word of those in the financial office and in an attempt to get me into school before the deadlines she committed to the loan under the same assumption as myself that the loan would be taken out in her name, the full details and breakdown of this loan was not provided to me because had it been provided and it was written that I also was taking a loan out in my name again I would have never moved forward.
