I have request to have an " in School Deferment '' of payments several times, via phone, with NENET, and with FASFA, and the request has incorrectly been denied. Under United States federal law, Title 34 of the Code of Federal Regulations ( CFR ), Section 685.204 ( b ) ( 2 ), a parent borrower who consolidates a Federal Parent PLUS loan into a Direct Consolidation Loan retains the absolute statutory right to defer repayment based on the undergraduate enrollment of the dependent student for whom the original funds were borrowed. The precise verbatim text from 34 CFR 685.204 ( b ) ( 2 ) states : A parent Direct PLUS Loan borrower is eligible for a deferment on a Direct PLUS Loan first disbursed on or after XX/XX/year> ( i ) Upon the request of the borrower, during the period when the student on whose behalf the loan was obtained is enrolled at an eligible institution on at least a half-time basis ; and ( ii ) Upon the request of the borrower, during the six-month period that begins on the later of the day after the student on whose behalf the loan was obtained ceases to be enrolled on at least a half-time basis...
