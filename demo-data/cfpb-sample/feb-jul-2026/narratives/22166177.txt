Complaint Summary : Aidvantage refused to provide my existing IDR payment count, despite multiple clear requests and despite the fact that the SAVE injunction does not prevent disclosure of existing data. They repeatedly cited the injunction as a reason they can not view my count, even after I clarified I was not requesting a future update. This is a servicing violation. Full Complaint : I requested my current IDR payment count from Aidvantage, specifically the count stored in their internal IDR tracking file, which includes repayment, forbearance, and deferment months accumulated under the IDR Account Adjustment. I made it clear that I was not requesting a future SAVE/RAP update. I requested only the existing count already on file, which servicers are required to disclose upon request. Aidvantage repeatedly responded with the SAVE injunction script, stating that IDR counts are temporarily unavailable. I clarified multiple times that the injunction only pauses future updates, not access to existing historical counts. Despite this, Aidvantage refused to provide my existing IDR count. Their final statement was : We are not able to provide any current IDR payment counters at this time. This is a refusal, not an inability. They did not state that the count does not exist, only that they will not provide it. This refusal violates borrower information rights and prevents me from verifying my eligibility for IDR forgiveness and refunds. I am requesting that the CFPB require Aidvantage to : Provide my current IDR payment count Provide the breakdown of counted repayment, forbearance, and deferment months Confirm whether my account has been reviewed under the IDR Account Adjustment Correct any servicing errors preventing access to my existing data I am attaching the full chat transcript showing the refusal.
