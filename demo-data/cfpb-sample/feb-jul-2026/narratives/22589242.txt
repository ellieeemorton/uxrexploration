I am submitting this complaint regarding Department of Education student loans that are currently reporting on my credit report as collection/charge-off accounts, which is completely inaccurate and deeply concerning. These accounts were not previously reporting, and I have no knowledge of them whatsoever. I did not take out these loans, I did not attend school, and I am not in any way associated with this debt. This is a serious issue that I believe may be the result of a reporting error or potentially mixed or fraudulent information being attached to my credit file. Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act ( FCRA ), any account listed on my credit report must be fully accurate, verifiable, and clearly connected to me, and at this time, these accounts do not meet those requirements. The sudden appearance of multiple student loan charge-offs that do not belong to me is extremely damaging to my credit and is a major violation of my rights as a consumer.
