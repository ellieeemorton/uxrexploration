I am submitting this complaint regarding the handling and protection of my personal and financial information associated with my federal student loan account serviced by Nelnet on behalf of the U.S. Department of Education. I became aware that my personal information was exposed in a federal student loan data/privacy breach involving information maintained or serviced through Nelnet and/or the Department of Education. The exposure of my information has caused significant concern regarding identity theft, financial harm, unauthorized account activity, and long-term damage to my personal credit and financial security. The exposed information may have included sensitive personally identifiable information such as my name, address, Social Security number, date of birth, account information, and other confidential financial records associated with my federal student loans. As a borrower, I entrusted Nelnet and the Department of Education with safeguarding my sensitive information. I believe they failed to maintain adequate security measures and failed to properly protect borrower data from unauthorized exposure or misuse. Because of this incident, I am requesting : 1. A full investigation into the exposure and handling of my personal information. 2. Written confirmation identifying exactly what information was exposed. 3. Copies of all records associated with my student loan account. 4. Removal or correction of any inaccurate reporting associated with my student loan accounts. 5. Protection from collection activity while this matter is investigated. 6. Review of my eligibility for discharge, cancellation, or other borrower relief due to the breach and resulting harm. 7. Assurance that my information will not continue to be improperly shared or exposed. This situation has caused stress, fear of identity theft, and concern regarding the integrity of my financial records and student loan account history. I request prompt investigation and resolution of this matter. Thank you.
