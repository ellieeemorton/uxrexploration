I am a federal student loan borrower serviced by Nelnet. I am submitting this complaint to document concerns regarding both ( 1 ) account handling during recent repayment plan changes and ( 2 ) a potential data security issue affecting my account. During the transition away from the Saving on a Valuable Education ( SAVE ) repayment plan, I experienced confusion and lack of clear, consistent communication regarding my repayment status and options. This included uncertainty around payment obligations, plan eligibility, and account status. The information provided was not sufficient for me to make informed financial decisions and created risk of financial harm. In addition, I have concerns regarding the security of my personal information. I have reason to believe that my account information may have been exposed or improperly handled during this period. While I have not yet received formal confirmation of a breach, the timing and nature of the issues raise concern about whether my personal dataincluding potentially sensitive identifying informationwas adequately protected. As a borrower, I rely on my loan servicer to provide accurate information and safeguard my personal data. Any failure in these areas creates significant risk, including identity theft, financial harm, and long-term credit impact. I am requesting the following : A written explanation of my account history during the recent repayment plan transition, including any changes to my repayment plan, payment status, and interest accrual. Confirmation as to whether my personal data has been involved in any data security incident, breach, or unauthorized access event. Details of any safeguards or remediation steps taken to protect affected borrowers. Correction of any inaccurate account information and assurance that my account has been properly updated. I am submitting this complaint to formally document these concerns and to ensure accountability, transparency, and proper handling of my account going forward.
