I am filing this complaint regarding the handling of my federal student loan account and the reported access to Department of Education systems by the Department of Government Efficiency ( DOGE ). I am concerned that my personally identifiable information and student loan records may have been accessed without my knowledge or consent. As a federal student loan borrower, I entrusted the Department of Education and its contractors with highly sensitive personal and financial information. Reports of broad access to borrower databases have raised serious concerns about whether my information was accessed, who had access, the legal authority for that access, and whether appropriate safeguards were followed. To date, I have not received clear or complete information addressing : * Whether my personal records were accessed. * Who accessed my information. * The legal authority permitting such access. * Whether the Privacy Act of 1974, the Federal Information Security Modernization Act ( FISMA ), and other applicable federal privacy and information security requirements were fully followed. * What protections or remedies are available if borrower information was improperly accessed or disclosed. I respectfully request that this matter be investigated and that the appropriate parties provide a written response identifying whether my records were affected and explaining the legal basis for any access to my information. If an investigation determines that my privacy rights were violated or that my personal information was improperly accessed, disclosed, or inadequately protected, I request that all remedies available under applicable law and agency authority be considered, including any administrative relief that may be available for affected borrowers. I also request that my student loan account be reviewed for any relief or discharge programs for which I may qualify if such violations are substantiated. I appreciate your prompt attention to this complaint and request a written response addressing my concerns and outlining any corrective actions being taken.
