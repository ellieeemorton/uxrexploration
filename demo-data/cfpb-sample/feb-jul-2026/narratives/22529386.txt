I am submitting this complaint regarding the reporting and servicing of my student loan accounts serviced by MOHELA. During the period when my student loan forbearance was ending and my loans were being transferred between servicers, I experienced significant confusion and lack of access regarding who serviced my loans and how to access my accounts for payment. I did not receive adequate or timely information allowing me to properly transition my accounts and resume payments without interruption. As a result, multiple late payments, including 120+ day late reporting, were added across several student loan accounts during the same concentrated time period. I dispute the accuracy and fairness of this reporting because : * I was actively attempting to determine the correct servicer and regain account access * I experienced difficulty accessing account information during the transfer period * Notices regarding the transfer and payment obligations were inadequate and/or untimely * The late reporting occurred during a narrow transition period rather than as a long-term pattern of nonpayment * My accounts are now current and in good standing I am requesting a full investigation into : * The servicing transfer timeline * All notices allegedly sent regarding transfer of servicing and end of forbearance * Account access availability during the transfer period * Payment posting history * Whether proper servicing procedures and notification requirements were followed * The accuracy of all late payment reporting during this transition period I also request review of whether these late payments were improperly reported during a period of servicing disruption and lack of reasonable account access. If errors, servicing failures, inadequate notice, or reporting inaccuracies are identified, I request correction or removal of the associated late payment reporting from all consumer reporting agencies. These derogatory marks are significantly impacting my credit profile and ability to qualify for a mortgage despite my accounts currently being in good standing.
