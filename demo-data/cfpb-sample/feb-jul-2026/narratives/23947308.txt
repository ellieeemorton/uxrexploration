My federal student loans were discharged under the U.S. Department of Education 's Borrower Defense to Repayment program after attending the XXXX XXXX XXXX XXXX XXXX. My loans now have a {$0.00} balance, and my National Student Loan Data System ( NSLDS ) records confirm that both of my Direct Consolidation Loans were discharged under Borrower Defense ( Discharge Code BD01 ). My NSLDS records also show that I made approximately {$15000.00} in payments toward these Direct Consolidation Loans prior to their discharge. Additionally, MOHELA 's account history reflects an adjustment of {$15000.00} on XX/XX/XXXX, which is consistent with the refund amount that should be returned following my Borrower Defense discharge. On XX/XX/XXXX, I submitted an IDQ request to MOHELA ( Reference # XXXX ) regarding my Borrower Defense refund. MOHELA subsequently informed me that, as a result of that request, a refund request had been submitted to the U.S. Department of the Treasury on XX/XX/XXXX. Because I had not received my refund months later, I contacted both Federal Student Aid ( FSA ) and the U.S. Department of the Treasury. Both agencies informed me they have no record of receiving any refund request from MOHELA on my behalf. After receiving this conflicting information, I sent MOHELA a written request asking for : Copies of any refund requests submitted to FSA and/or the U.S. Department of the Treasury ; Documentation showing when the refund request was transmitted ; Written confirmation of the refund amount and current status ; and An explanation of why FSA and Treasury had no record of the submission MOHELA claimed had been made. MOHELA responded on XX/XX/XXXX, stating that : My account was being manually reviewed ; My refund request was being expedited ; A letter containing the requested information had been requested ; and I should receive that letter within approximately XXXX business days. None of those commitments were fulfilled. As of today : I have not received my refund ; I have not received the promised letter ; I have not received any documentation showing that a refund request was ever submitted ; I have not received any explanation for why both FSA and the U.S. Treasury have no record of the refund request MOHELA claimed was submitted ; and I continue to receive conflicting and inconsistent information regarding the status of my refund. This matter has now been ongoing for well over two years. Despite repeated requests, MOHELA has failed to provide documentation supporting its representations or explain the discrepancies between its statements and the information provided by Federal Student Aid and the U.S. Department of the Treasury. I am requesting CFPB assistance because this complaint is not simply about a delayed refund, it is about MOHELA 's failure to accurately process, document, and communicate the status of my Borrower Defense refund while providing inconsistent information over an extended period of time.
