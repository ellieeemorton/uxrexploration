I am not disputing that these loans belong to me. I signed the promissory notes, acknowledge the debt, and want to repay my loans. My complaint concerns whether my loans were serviced appropriately before negative information was reported to the nationwide consumer reporting agencies. I did not receive billing statements or repayment notices by mail at my residence. While I understand that some communications may have been sent electronically, I am requesting that Nelnet provide a complete record of every billing statement, repayment notice, delinquency notice, email, and other communication they rely upon to support their reporting. I also request that Nelnet identify : * The mailing address they had on file throughout the life of the loans. * The email address they used. * The dates each billing statement and repayment notice was sent. * Whether any mailed notices were returned as undeliverable. * The basis on which they concluded I had been adequately notified before reporting late payments to the credit bureaus. My dispute is not about avoiding repayment. My concern is whether I received adequate and effective notice before my credit history was negatively affected. If Nelnet can not demonstrate that the required notices were properly sent and that the servicing of my account complied with applicable requirements, I request that they take appropriate corrective action, including correcting any inaccurate or improper credit reporting if warranted. I am requesting a thorough investigation and a complete explanation supported by documentation. I was never told it was going to collections on my credit report. I sent 3 screenshots showing how fast my credit crashed due to my loans not being serviced appropriately. I spent years fixing my credit and in 3 months it fell 200 points. I never received any kind of bill to my address or any collection notices to my mailing address.
