My federal student loans were in an approved forbearance status. When the forbearance period ended, MOHELA failed to notify me in any way no letter, no email, no phone call, and no other communication. I received zero notice that payments were resuming and was given no opportunity to make payments or enroll in an income-driven repayment plan before delinquency was reported. I had no knowledge that my account was delinquent until I applied for a mortgage pre-approval for a home purchase. The lender informed me during that process that my credit report showed over 290 days of student loan late payments, which disqualified me from obtaining the loan. That mortgage application was the first time I became aware of any delinquency. MOHELA never contacted me before, during, or after the forbearance ended. The 290+ days of delinquency reported to all three credit bureaus are entirely the result of MOHELA 's failure to communicate with me, not willful non-payment. I have always been willing and able to make payments. I was never told I needed to resume them. This has caused direct, concrete financial harm : I was denied a mortgage pre-approval solely because of these erroneous delinquency marks. The Department of Education and federal oversight bodies have publicly documented that MOHELA systematically failed to notify borrowers when COVID-era forbearance ended. My situation is a direct result of that documented, systemic failure.
