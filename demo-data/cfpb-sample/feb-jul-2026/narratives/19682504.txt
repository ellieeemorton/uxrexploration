I am writing to formally file a complaint regarding the handling of my federal student loan account by MOHELA. In 2024, I submitted an Income-Driven Repayment ( IDR ) application in good faith and within the required timeframe. However, I have since been informed that this application was never processed. I was not notified of any deficiencies, missing documentation, or errors at the time of submission. As a result, my repayment status and eligibility for income-based relief were negatively impacted. After becoming aware that my original application had not been processed, I submitted a new IDR application. Since that time, I have received conflicting information regarding its status. One communication indicated that my application was denied, while my online account and/or other communications reflect that it remains under review. Despite multiple attempts to seek clarification, I have not received consistent guidance regarding the status of my application or instructions on what corrective action, if any, is required. This lack of transparency and inconsistent communication has caused significant confusion and financial uncertainty. Borrowers depend on accurate processing of IDR applications to ensure affordable payments and maintain compliance with federal loan requirements. The failure to process my initial application and the contradictory information regarding my current submission reflect serious servicing deficiencies. Additionally, I am deeply concerned about reports of servicing failures and potential data security issues. Given the sensitive personal and financial information entrusted to MOHELA, any confirmed security breach or mishandling of borrower data would raise serious concerns regarding account management, consumer protection, and institutional oversight.
