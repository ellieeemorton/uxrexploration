I am submitting a complaint regarding ongoing issues with Aidvantages loan servicing platform, specifically related to account access, transparency of loan information, and inconsistent balance disclosures. Since entering repayment, I have experienced repeated difficulty logging into my account. Access is inconsistent and unreliable, which limits my ability to monitor my loan status. When I am able to access my account, the platform does not clearly display total accrued interest across all loans. Instead, interest is only shown on a per-loan basis, requiring me to manually navigate through multiple screens and calculate totals myself. There is no clear, consolidated view of total interest owed. Additionally, there are discrepancies between the balance displayed in my account and the payoff amount provided. For example, my account may show a balance of approximately {$1700.00} on a loan, but when I request a payoff quote, the amount increases significantly ( e.g., approximately {$4000.00} ). This lack of transparency makes it difficult to understand my true financial obligation. I have attempted to get clarification, but responses from Aidvantage have not resolved the issue and have not provided clear explanations. Impact : These issues have caused ongoing confusion, stress, and an inability to confidently understand or manage my loan repayment. As a borrower, I should be able to easily access accurate, complete, and transparent information about my loan balance and interest. Requested Resolution : I am requesting that Aidvantage : Provide a clear and consistent method to access my account without login issues Display total accrued interest across all loans in a single, easily accessible location Ensure that displayed balances and payoff amounts are transparent and clearly explained Improve the usability of their platform so borrowers can easily understand their obligations without excessive navigation.
