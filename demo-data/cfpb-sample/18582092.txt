Subject : Complaint Regarding Payment Application, Due Date Advancement, and Servicing Issues After Transfer from Great Lakes I am submitting this complaint regarding the handling of my federal student loans following the servicing transfer from Great Lakes to Nelnet. 

At the time of the transfer, I was informed that the terms and handling of my loans would remain the same. While my interest rates and repayment plan ( Extended Repayment ) did not change, the application and presentation of my payments has become materially more complex, inconsistent, and difficult to understand. 

Specifically : I consistently pay more than my required monthly payment. 
I selected the option to limit due date advancement to no more than one month. 
Despite this, my billing statements continue to reflect paid-ahead credits, fluctuating amount due figures, and loan groups with widely divergent due dates. 
Excess payments appear to be applied as partial future credits or distributed across loan groups rather than clearly and consistently reducing principal in a predictable manner. 

In addition, I have contacted Nelnet customer service multiple times in an effort to understand and resolve these issues. These calls have often required significant time commitments, including extended call durations, and have not resulted in a clear or consistent explanation of how excess payments are applied or how my payment preferences are implemented across loan groups. Despite these efforts, I remain unable to obtain a definitive resolution or confirmation that my instructions are being fully honored. 

I am not alleging incorrect interest rates, missing payments, or unauthorized charges. However, the current servicing behavior represents a significant decline in transparency and usability compared to my experience prior to the transfer. The lack of clear, consistent explanations and predictable payment application makes it difficult for borrowers to verify progress, evaluate interest impact, and ensure that excess payments are reducing long-term costs as intended. 

I respectfully request : 1. Written clarification of how excess payments are applied when due date advancement is limited.

2. Confirmation of how a borrower can ensure excess payments are applied directly to principal without being treated as paid-ahead credits.

3. Review and confirmation that my current payment preferences are being fully honored across all loan groups.

4. Consideration of whether current servicer communications and support adequately explain these behaviors to borrowers following servicer transfers. 

I appreciate your review and request a written response for my records. 

Thank you for your time and attention.
