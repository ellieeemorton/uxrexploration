# Transcript P3 — Oceanside Loan Servicing, Repayment Plan Comparison Study

Session: 40 min, remote video call, screen-share
Participant: P3 — 26, junior graphic designer, about $27,000 remaining, in repayment for two years
Moderator: M

---

## Part 1: Background

**M:** Okay, so, recording's going. Thanks for joining. Can you tell me a little about your loans?

**P3:** Um, sure. I have, like, federal loans. From college. I graduated two years ago.

**M:** And do you know roughly how much you have left?

**P3:** Twenty-seven, I think? Maybe twenty-six-something.

**M:** Okay. And are you on a particular plan?

**P3:** I think it's the standard one. It's what they put me on.

**M:** Have you ever looked at changing it?

**P3:** Not really. No.

**M:** Okay. Um, how come?

**P3:** I don't know. I just, it's fine, I guess. The payment's okay. I didn't want to mess it up.

**M:** Mess it up how?

**P3:** Like, click the wrong thing. And it changes. And then I'm on some plan I didn't want.

**M:** That makes sense. Has that happened, or is that more of a worry?

**P3:** More of a worry. I read on a forum that somebody did that. It was a different company though.

**M:** Okay. Um. And how often do you log in to Oceanside?

**P3:** Like, every few months. To check stuff.

**M:** What stuff?

**P3:** My balance. And, like, that my payment went through.

**M:** Great. So let's go ahead and have you share your screen. I'm going to ask you to go to the plan comparison page and work out which plan you'd pick, but you won't be submitting anything. It's not real.

**P3:** Okay. Sure. Um, how do I share?

**M:** There should be a button at the bottom of the call window. Green one.

**P3:** Oh, found it.

---

## Part 2: Screen-share task

[P3 shares her full screen rather than one window. Several personal tabs and a chat app are briefly visible. She notices and says "oh, sorry," and closes the chat. The moderator says it's no problem.]

**M:** Go ahead and log in and go to, um, Compare Plans. And just talk through whatever you're thinking.

**P3:** Okay.

[P3 logs in. Dashboard loads. She looks at the screen for about eight seconds without moving the cursor.]

**P3:** Um. Is it under "Repayment"?

**M:** What do you think?

**P3:** I think so.

[P3 clicks "Payments" in the top nav instead. A payments page loads. She looks at it for about five seconds, then clicks back.]

**P3:** Oh, no. That's for paying.

[P3 clicks "Repayment." Dropdown appears. She reads the items for about six seconds and clicks "Compare Plans."]

**P3:** Okay. There it is.

[Comparison page loads. P3 scrolls down to the bottom of the table, then back up to the top. Scrolls down again and stops at the income estimator. Scrolls back up.]

**M:** What are you looking at?

**P3:** Just the plans. There's four.

**M:** What stands out to you?

**P3:** Um. They have different numbers. The monthly payment ones.

[P3 reads the monthly payment column, moving the cursor down the column and up again. Clicks on the "Payoff date" column header. Nothing happens because the header is not clickable. She clicks it again.]

**P3:** Oh, it doesn't do anything.

**M:** That's alright. What were you hoping it would do?

**P3:** I thought it might sort it. Or explain it.

[P3 hovers over the "i" icon next to "Total estimated paid." Tooltip appears. She reads it, then moves the cursor off so the tooltip disappears, then hovers again and reads it a second time.]

**M:** What did you think of that?

**P3:** It was fine. It says it includes interest.

[P3 scrolls down to the income estimator. Clicks into the income field. Types 52000. Pauses about ten seconds. Deletes it, types 52,000 with the comma. Clicks "Update estimate." A small red error appears: "Enter numbers only." P3 deletes the comma, retypes 52000, clicks the button again. Table updates.]

**M:** How did that go?

**P3:** It was fine. It just didn't like the comma.

**M:** Did that throw you off?

**P3:** A little. But it was fine.

[P3 scrolls up to the table and reads the Income-Linked Flex row. Scrolls back down to the estimator and reads the label, "Estimate with your income," then scrolls up to the table. Reads the Income-Linked row again. Does this back-and-forth twice more.]

**M:** What's going on there? I see you going up and down.

**P3:** Oh. I just wanted to see if the numbers changed.

**M:** And did they?

**P3:** I think so? The income one is different. It was, like, a hundred and something, and now it's a bit more.

**M:** Okay. Do you understand why it changed?

**P3:** Because I put in my income.

**M:** Right. And, what do you think "household size" is for?

**P3:** Um. Probably how many people live with you. I left it at one.

[P3 had not interacted with the household size field. It is still set to the default of one. She looks at the field for about four seconds.]

**M:** Okay. So which plan would you choose, would you say?

**P3:** I think the, um. Standard. Since I'm on it.

**M:** Is that because you like it, or because it's the one you know?

**P3:** I guess because it's the one I know. The others I'm not really sure what they mean. Like "Income-Linked Flex." What's flex?

**M:** What do you think it could mean?

**P3:** That it changes? Maybe? But I don't know how.

[P3 scrolls to the top and reads the plan names in the first column, left to right, then the plan descriptions under each name. She reads the Graduated description twice. Moves her mouth as if reading along.]

**P3:** It's fine. I get it.

**M:** What do you get?

**P3:** That the payment goes up over time. For that one.

**M:** Right.

[P3 clicks "Select plan" on the Standard row. A confirmation screen appears. She reads it for about fifteen seconds. Scrolls up. Scrolls down. Reads it again. Hovers over the "Confirm" button, does not click, and clicks "Back."]

**M:** What were you thinking there?

**P3:** I just didn't want to actually press it.

**M:** Right, it's not real, but, yeah. Was the confirmation screen clear?

**P3:** It was fine.

**P3:** The monthly payment. Because that's what I pay.

**M:** Okay. And did you trust the numbers?

**P3:** I think so. They matched what I pay for the standard plan. So I guess the others are probably right too.

**M:** Anything you'd want to see that's not there?

**P3:** Because then I'd know I wasn't doing it wrong.

**M:** Okay. That's a good point. Thanks so much, P3, this was really helpful.

**P3:** Oh, okay. Good. Was that, did I do okay?

**M:** You did great. There's no right answer.

**P3:** Okay. Cool.

[Session ends. Total time approximately 26 minutes.]
