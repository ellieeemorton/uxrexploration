L1: Complaint to Consumer Financial Protection Bureau ( CFPB ) Subject : Servicer Refusal to Provide Written Response or Substantiate Claim Central Research , Inc . I am submitting a follow-up complaint regarding Central Research , Inc. ( CRI ) and the servicing of my federal student loan. Summary of Issue CRI previously acknowledged that my repayment plan was incorrectly configured and subsequently increased my monthly payment. They have also stated that this error did not result in any additional interest or financial impact. Current Problem I have made multiple written requests for a specific calculation to verify this claim, including : - A comparison of total repayment under the corrected schedule versus what I would have paid if the correct schedule had been applied from the beginning - The methodology used to calculate that comparison CRI has not provided this information. Instead, they have repeatedly responded with a generic message stating that they are unable to discuss account specifics via email and directing me to call or use chat. Why This Is a Problem- I am making a clear, specific, and reasonable request directly related to a previously acknowledged servicing error - CRI has made a quantitative claim about lack of financial impact but refuses to provide the calculation supporting that claim- CRI is refusing to provide a substantive written response, preventing me from maintaining accurate records or verifying their statements Requested Resolution I am requesting that CRI be required to : 1. Provide a written response addressing my request 2. Provide the specific calculation necessary to verify their claim that I was not financially impacted 3. Substantiate their conclusion with sufficient detail to allow independent verification -- - At this point, the issue is not a lack of communication from my side, but a refusal by the servicer to provide a written, substantive response to a clearly defined request.
