L1: I am filing this complaint regarding a federal student loan debt that I have disputed for several years. Approximately eighteen months ago, I submitted a written dispute to the servicer by both mail and fax. Along with my dispute, I included the servicer 's own form requesting validation and documentation supporting the alleged debt. The correspondence clearly contained my current mailing address, which remains my address today. I have also maintained the same telephone number for approximately twenty-five years. The servicer received my dispute but never provided a response, never supplied the requested documentation, and never addressed the issues raised in my dispute. Instead, the servicer simply ignored my correspondence. Recently, my elderly mother informed me that the servicer mailed a letter to my father stating that it was attempting to locate me and requesting my current address. This statement is false. The servicer already possessed my current address because I provided it in my written dispute, and it has not changed. The servicer also had multiple methods to contact me directly. What makes this conduct especially troubling is that my father suffers from XXXX XXXX XXXX. Rather than communicating with me using the contact information already in its possession, the servicer chose to contact my family members and represent that it did not know how to reach me. This was unnecessary, misleading, and caused distress to my family. I believe the servicer 's actions demonstrate a failure to properly investigate and respond to a borrower dispute and may constitute unfair or deceptive acts or practices under the Consumer Financial Protection Act. The servicer 's representation that it could not locate me appears inaccurate given that it had my current address and telephone number. Its decision to contact third parties despite possessing my contact information raises serious concerns regarding its servicing practices and recordkeeping procedures. I am requesting that the CFPB require the servicer to : Conduct a full investigation of my dispute ; Provide all documents and records supporting the existence, ownership, and amount of the alleged debt ; Explain why it represented to third parties that it could not locate me despite having my correct address and telephone number ; Cease unnecessary third-party contacts regarding me ; Review whether its handling of my dispute complied with applicable federal consumer protection laws and student loan servicing requirements ; and Correct any inaccurate records maintained regarding my account. Attached are copies of the dispute letters, forms, fax confirmations, and mailing records showing that the servicer received my dispute and had my correct address well before it contacted my family members seeking information it already possessed. The servicer 's failure to respond to my dispute and subsequent contact with my family members appear designed to pressure and harass rather than resolve a legitimate borrower dispute. I respectfully request that the CFPB investigate these practices and require a substantive response from the servicer.
