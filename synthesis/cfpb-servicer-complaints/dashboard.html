Servicer Complaint Patterns

1,948 real CFPB student loan servicing complaints, February–July 2026, across 54 companies.

This is real, public CFPB complaint data — unlike the Oceanside repayment-plan study in this same project, which is synthetic. Quant breakdowns below cover the full population of 1,948 complaints. The qualitative read of open-ended narratives (synthesis/cfpb-servicer-complaints/) covers a stratified random sample of 99, documented in sample-manifest.md — pending researcher review before theme-building.
